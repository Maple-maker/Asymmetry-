**Bear Case:**

There's a risk of operational missteps, downtime, or failure to meet stringent hyperscaler SLAs (Service Level Agreements), which could damage their reputation and limit future contract opportunities. The speed at which they can acquire, deploy, and configure high-demand GPUs, which are themselves supply-constrained, is also a critical dependency that they don't fully control.
