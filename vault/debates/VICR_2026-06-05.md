## Vicor Corporation (VICR) Analysis: Catalysts and Risks

**Company:** Vicor Corporation (VICR)
**Sector signal source:** XAI
**Thesis:** Vicor is the leading designer of high-density, high-efficiency power modules (BCM, DCM, PRM) that are essential for liquid-cooled AI racks. Elon Musk’s need for “every liquid cooling solution” includes the power delivery infrastructure that must operate at 48V or 400V with extreme efficiency to avoid heat waste in immersion cooling. Vicor’s “Factorized Power Architecture” is used by major AI server OEMs (e.g., Dell, Supermicro) to power NVIDIA H100/B200 GPUs in liquid-cooled environments. Vicor’s revenue is directly tied to the density of AI clusters.
**Catalyst:** Q4 2024 earnings (February 2025) – guidance on “AI cluster power module shipments” and capacity expansion for 100kW+ racks.
