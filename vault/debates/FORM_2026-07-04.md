## FormFactor, Inc. (FORM) Analysis: Catalysts and Risks

**Sector Signal Source:** AMZN (Increased demand for HBM-enabled AI GPUs)

**Thesis:** FormFactor is a leading supplier of advanced probe cards and test sockets used in the final test of HBM memory stacks and AI GPU packages. As Amazon buys more Nvidia H200/B200 GPUs and SK Hynix/Samsung HBM3E, the test interface market tightens. FormFactor’s proprietary MEMS-based probe cards are critical for yield management in high-bandwidth memory, and they have direct revenue from HBM test solutions.
