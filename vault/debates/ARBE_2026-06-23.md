**Catalyst:** Q4 2024 earnings (March 2025) where any design win with Qualcomm or tier-one automotive supplier will be disclosed.

As autonomous driving levels advance, regulatory bodies may increasingly mandate higher performance and reliability for sensor suites. Broader industry adoption or regulatory push for higher resolution radar (like Arbe's 4D imaging radar) as a critical component for L2+/L3+ autonomous driving could accelerate demand and design wins.

Source: ARBE 10-K filed March 28, 2023 (Item 1A. Risk Factors - "We face intense competition from many sources, which could harm our business.").
