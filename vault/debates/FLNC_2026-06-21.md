RED_FLAG_2_SEVERITY: MEDIUM
RED_FLAG_2: **Supply Chain Volatility and Raw Material Costs** — The battery storage industry is heavily reliant on critical raw materials (lithium, nickel, cobalt). Geopolitical tensions, mining constraints, and increasing demand can lead to significant price volatility and supply chain disruptions. While Fluence has diverse supplier relationships, sustained high input costs could erode profitability or cause project delays. — Source: Fluence Q1 2024 Earnings Call Transcript (Feb 14, 2024), 10-Q Filing Q1 2024 (Feb 14, 2024)

RED_FLAG_3_SEVERITY: MEDIUM
RED_FLAG_3: **Project Execution Risk and Warranty Liabilities** — Large-scale energy storage projects are complex, with long lead times and significant capital requirements. Fluence faces risks related to project delays, cost overruns, and performance issues. Additionally, as an integrator, they carry warranty liabilities for the performance of the systems they deliver, which could lead to significant expenses if components fail or underperform. — Source: Fluence 10-K Filing FY2023 (Dec 13, 2023) - "Risk Factors" section detailing project completion and warranty obligations.

### BEAR_VERDICT:
The bull thesis holds, as the identified catalysts, particularly the Amazon contract, represent significant growth opportunities, though the company must effectively navigate intense competition, supply chain challenges, and inherent project execution risks to realize its full potential.
