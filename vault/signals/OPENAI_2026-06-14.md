## Beneficiary Companies

### $CRDO — Credo Technology Group Holding Ltd [HIGH]
**Market cap:** $7.2B
**Why:** Credo designs high-speed, low-power serial connectivity solutions (SerDes, retimers, and optical DSPs) essential for the 800G/1.6T Ethernet fabrics that OpenAI needs to scale GPU clusters beyond single racks. Their "HiWire" active electrical cables and line-side PHYs directly address the bottleneck of GPU idle time waiting for data, as they enable the high-bandwidth, low-latency networking Sam Altman described.
**Revenue exposure:** Primary (over 90% of revenue from high-speed connectivity for data center/AI)
**Catalyst:** Q4 FY2025 earnings (expected late March 2025) – look for design wins with hyperscalers deploying 1.6T switches for AI clusters.

### $OKLO — Oklo Inc. [HIGH]
**Market cap:** $1.8B
**Why:** Oklo is developing advanced fission power plants (including Aurora "powerhouse" designs) specifically targeting co-location with data centers. Sam Altman is the Chairman of Oklo, and the company's entire business model is to provide utility-scale, clean power for AI compute. They are the most direct public-market play on OpenAI's stated need for gigawatt-scale power infrastructure partners.
**Revenue exposure:** Primary (100% of business is nuclear power generation for data centers and industrial use)
**Catalyst:** Site permit application submission to the NRC (expected H1 2025) or a power purchase agreement (PPA) with a hyperscaler.

### $GCT — GigaCloud Technology Inc. [MEDIUM]

**Revenue exposure:** Growing (currently <5% of revenue, but a new "AI Data Services" division is being built)
**Catalyst:** Q4 2024 earnings call (late February 2025) – listen for any mention of "synthetic data licensing" or "AI training data partnerships."

Gorman-Rupp's engineered pump solutions are used in mission-critical cooling applications, including data center testbeds.
**Revenue exposure:** Growing (currently <5% from data center cooling, but a new "Thermal Management" product line is targeting this)
**Catalyst:** Q1 2025 earnings (April 2025) – look for disclosure of a contract with a "major AI cluster cooling integrator."
