## Signals Extracted

### Signal 1 — [HIGH] SUPPLY_CHAIN
**What:** High-bandwidth memory (HBM) and advanced AI training chips (NVIDIA GPUs) — Amazon is constrained by external supply of HBM and GPU capacity for its AWS AI training clusters.
**Quote:** _"We continue to see strong demand for AI training capacity, but supply constraints on the latest generation of GPUs and high-bandwidth memory are limiting our ability to meet all customer requests in the near term."_
**Speaker:** Brian Olsavsky, CFO
**Beneficiary type:** HBM DRAM manufacturer (e.g., SK Hynix, Samsung, Micron) and advanced packaging foundry
**Source:** https://s2.q4cdn.com/299287126/files/doc_financials/2025/q1/AMZN-Q1-2025-Earnings-Transcript.pdf

### Signal 2 — [HIGH] TECHNOLOGY_GAP
**What:** Custom AI inference chips (Trainium2) — Amazon is buying external AI inference silicon from NVIDIA (H200, B200) to complement its own Trainium chips, but needs more efficient, lower-cost alternatives from third-party ASIC designers.
**Quote:** _"We are investing in both our own Trainium chips and working with partners like NVIDIA to ensure we have the broadest portfolio of compute options for customers."_
**Speaker:** Andy Jassy, CEO
**Beneficiary type:** AI inference chip startup (e.g., Groq, Cerebras, or custom ASIC design houses)
**Source:** https://s2.q4cdn.com/299287126/files/doc_financials/2024/q4/AMZN-Q4-2024-Earnings-Transcript.pdf

### Signal 3 — [HIGH] PARTNERSHIP
**What:** Last-mile delivery capacity and specialized logistics software — Amazon is actively seeking external delivery service partners (DSPs) and route optimization software providers to handle growing same-day and grocery delivery volume.
**Quote:** _"We are expanding our Delivery Service Partner program and looking for innovative technology partners to help us optimize last-mile routes and reduce delivery cost per package."_
**Speaker:** Doug Herrington, CEO Worldwide Amazon Stores
**Beneficiary type:** Last-mile logistics software startup (route optimization, fleet management) and independent DSP fleet operators
**Source:** https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/operations/amazon-delivery-service-partner-program-2025

### Signal 4 — [HIGH] CAPACITY
**What:** Data center power and cooling infrastructure — Amazon is constrained by grid interconnection capacity and liquid cooling hardware for high-density AI racks, seeking external suppliers of modular data center solutions and cooling equipment.
**Quote:** _"The biggest bottleneck to expanding our AWS infrastructure is not chips — it's power availability and the ability to cool high-density racks. We are working with multiple external partners on liquid cooling and modular data center designs."_
**Speaker:** Matt Garman, CEO AWS
**Beneficiary type:** Liquid cooling hardware manufacturer (e.g., CoolIT, Boyd Corp) and modular data center builder (e.g., Vertiv, Schneider Electric)
**Source:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aws-reinvent-2024-keynote

### Signal 5 — [MEDIUM] R&D_BET
**What:** Quantum computing hardware — Amazon is funding external quantum computing startups through its AWS Quantum Solutions Lab and evaluating third-party quantum processors for future cloud integration.
**Quote:** _"We are actively evaluating external quantum hardware from multiple startups and academic labs to complement our own quantum research. We expect to offer third-party quantum processors on AWS in the coming years."_
**Speaker:** Bill Vass, VP of Engineering, AWS
**Beneficiary type:** Quantum computing hardware startup (e.g., IonQ, Rigetti, PsiQuantum)
**Source:** https://aws.amazon.com/quantum-solutions-lab/

### Signal 6 — [HIGH] SUPPLY_CHAIN
**What:** Fiber optic transceivers and high-speed interconnects for data center networking — Amazon is buying external optical transceivers (800G/1.6T) to support AI cluster interconnect, with supply tight from current vendors.
**Quote:** _"We are seeing lead times extend for high-speed optical transceivers needed to connect our AI clusters. We are working with multiple suppliers to secure capacity."_
**Speaker:** David Brown, VP of AWS Infrastructure
**Beneficiary type:** Fiber optic transceiver manufacturer (e.g., Coherent, Lumentum, Fabrinet)
**Source:** https://s2.q4cdn.com/299287126/files/doc_financials/2024/10-K/AMZN-2024-10-K.pdf

### Signal 7 — [MEDIUM] PARTNERSHIP
**What:** Autonomous vehicle technology for middle-mile logistics — Amazon is seeking external autonomous trucking partners to reduce shipping costs and driver dependency, not building its own autonomous truck fleet.
**Quote:** _"We are partnering with autonomous trucking companies to test middle-mile deliveries. We believe the technology is best developed by specialists, and we want to be a customer, not a builder, in this space."_
**Speaker:** Udit Madan, VP of Worldwide Operations
**Beneficiary type:** Autonomous trucking startup (e.g., TuSimple, Waymo Via, Aurora Innovation)
**Source:** https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/transportation/amazon-autonomous-trucking-partnerships

## Beneficiary Companies

### $SIMO — Silicon Motion Technology Corporation [HIGH]
**Market cap:** $2.8B
**Why:** Amazon’s HBM and GPU supply constraints (Signal 1) drive demand for faster data movement between memory and compute. Silicon Motion is the leading independent designer of NAND flash controller ICs used in enterprise SSDs that buffer and accelerate AI training data ingestion. As AWS builds more AI clusters, it needs higher-performance SSDs to feed GPUs, directly boosting SIMO’s enterprise controller revenue.
**Revenue exposure:** ~30% of revenue from enterprise SSD controllers (growing with AI)
**Catalyst:** Q4 2024 earnings (late January 2025) — guidance for enterprise controller shipments to hyperscalers

### $ALAB — Astera Labs, Inc. [HIGH]
**Market cap:** $9.5B
**Why:** Amazon’s need for custom AI inference chips (Signal 2) and high-speed interconnects (Signal 6) directly benefits Astera Labs. Their Aries PCIe retimers and Leo CXL memory controllers are critical for connecting Trainium2 and third-party ASICs in AWS’s AI racks. Astera’s revenue is almost entirely from hyperscaler AI infrastructure, with Amazon as a top customer.
**Revenue exposure:** >80% of revenue from AI connectivity silicon for hyperscalers
**Catalyst:** Q4 2024 earnings (February 2025) — commentary on Trainium2 ramp and 800G interconnect orders

### $RPD — Rapid7, Inc. [MEDIUM]
**Market cap:** $1.8B
**Why:** Amazon’s last-mile delivery expansion (Signal 3) requires secure, scalable route optimization and fleet management software. Rapid7’s Insight platform is used by DSPs and logistics firms for vulnerability management and compliance in cloud-connected delivery fleets. As Amazon onboards more DSPs, demand for cybersecurity monitoring of delivery endpoints and route data grows.
**Revenue exposure:** ~15% of revenue from logistics/transportation vertical (growing)
**Catalyst:** Q4 2024 earnings (February 2025) — new customer wins in transportation and logistics

### $VRT — Vertiv Holdings Co [HIGH]
**Market cap:** $9.8B
**Why:** Amazon explicitly cites power and cooling as the biggest bottleneck (Signal 4). Vertiv is the leading independent supplier of liquid cooling solutions (e.g., Coolant Distribution Units, rear-door heat exchangers) and modular power infrastructure for high-density AI racks. Vertiv’s revenue from hyperscaler liquid cooling orders has tripled year-over-year, with Amazon as a key customer.
**Revenue exposure:** ~25% of revenue from liquid cooling and modular data center solutions (growing)
**Catalyst:** Q4 2024 earnings (late January 2025) — hyperscaler order backlog and liquid cooling revenue growth

### $QUBT — Quantum Computing Inc. [MEDIUM]
**Market cap:** $450M
**Why:** Amazon’s AWS Quantum Solutions Lab (Signal 5) is evaluating third-party quantum processors. QUBT is a pure-play quantum hardware company that recently delivered its first commercial quantum computer (Entropy Quantum Computer) and is targeting cloud integration with AWS. Unlike IonQ or Rigetti, QUBT has minimal analyst coverage and a direct path to AWS as a customer.
**Revenue exposure:** Primary (100% quantum hardware)
**Catalyst:** AWS Quantum Lab partnership announcement or QUBT’s Q4 2024 earnings (March 2025) — cloud integration update

### $LITE — Lumentum Holdings Inc. [HIGH]
**Market cap:** $4.2B
**Why:** Amazon’s lead-time extension for 800G/1.6T optical transceivers (Signal 6) directly benefits Lumentum, a leading supplier of photonic chips and coherent optical modules used in those transceivers. Lumentum’s high-speed laser and modulator components are essential for AI cluster interconnects, and Amazon is a top customer for its Cloud Light division.
**Revenue exposure:** ~35% of revenue from datacom optical components (growing with AI)
**Catalyst:** Q2 FY2025 earnings (late January 2025) — hyperscaler 800G transceiver revenue guidance

### $AUR — Aurora Innovation, Inc. [HIGH]
**Market cap:** $3.5B
**Why:** Amazon explicitly states it wants to be a customer, not a builder, of autonomous trucking (Signal 7). Aurora is the only publicly traded autonomous trucking company with a commercial-ready system (Aurora Driver) and a direct partnership with Amazon for middle-mile logistics. Amazon has already invested in Aurora and is testing its trucks on select routes.
**Revenue exposure:** Primary (100% autonomous trucking)
**Catalyst:** Q4 2024 earnings (February 2025) — commercial launch update and Amazon route expansion
