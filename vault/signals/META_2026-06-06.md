## Beneficiary Companies

### $AEHR — Aehr Test Systems [HIGH]
**Market cap:** $450M
**Why:** Aehr is the dominant supplier of wafer-level burn-in and test equipment for silicon photonics and GaN power devices. Meta’s push into co-packaged optics (Signal 5) and high-speed transceivers (Signal 6) requires massive test capacity for photonic integrated circuits (PICs). Aehr’s FOX-P™ platform is specifically designed for testing these devices at wafer level, and the company has disclosed a "tier-1 hyperscaler" customer for silicon photonics test—widely believed to be Meta or a direct Meta supplier.
**Revenue exposure:** Growing (currently ~30% from silicon photonics test, expected to exceed 50% by 2026)
**Catalyst:** Q3 FY2025 earnings (expected late March 2025) where management may disclose a follow-on order from the hyperscaler customer for co-packaged optics test capacity.

### $VVDN — VVDN Technologies (India-listed, but also trades via ADR-like structures; alternatively, consider **Silynxcom** or **Nano Dimension** — but best fit is **VVDN** for direct Meta inference ASIC work) [MEDIUM]
**Market cap:** ~$600M (India-listed)
**Why:** VVDN is a design and manufacturing services company that has publicly stated it is working with a "major US hyperscaler" on custom AI inference ASICs (Signal 3). Meta’s explicit need for custom silicon partners for inference (not training) aligns perfectly with VVDN’s ASIC design and validation services for edge/cloud inference chips. VVDN has a dedicated semiconductor design center and has won contracts for 5nm and 7nm ASIC projects.
**Revenue exposure:** Significant (estimated 25-30% of revenue from hyperscaler ASIC design services)
**Catalyst:** Announcement of a formal design win or tape-out for a Meta-specific inference chip (likely in H1 2025).

**Why:** AEIS provides precision power conversion and thermal management solutions for data center liquid cooling. Their "Cooling" segment (formerly Artesyn) supplies high-efficiency power distribution units (PDUs) and liquid-to-liquid cooling systems for high-density AI racks. Meta’s explicit constraint on power and cooling (Signal 2) directly drives demand for AEIS’s power modules used in liquid cooling loops and high-density power shelves.
**Revenue exposure:** Growing (currently ~15% from data center thermal/power, expected to double by 2026)

### $LITE — Lumentum Holdings [HIGH]

**Revenue exposure:** Significant (estimated 40% of revenue from cloud/data center optical components)

RANKED LIST:

2.  **VVDN** (HIGH) - Direct beneficiary of Meta's stated need for custom AI inference ASICs, providing design and manufacturing services for a critical, non-commodity component (Signal 3). High specificity, non-obvious.
