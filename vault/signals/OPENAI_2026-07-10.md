**Revenue exposure:** Primary (~100% of revenue from AI data center connectivity silicon)

**Revenue exposure:** Primary (~100% of revenue potential from SMR deployment for data centers)

### $FORM — FormFactor, Inc. [HIGH]
**Market cap:** ~$3.2B
**Why:** FormFactor provides advanced probe cards and test interfaces for HBM3 and advanced packaging (CoWoS) yield optimization. As HBM3 and CoWoS are the primary bottlenecks for GPU clusters, FormFactor's test solutions are critical for ramping production capacity, directly benefiting from OpenAI's demand for more memory and packaging.
**Revenue exposure:** Significant (~50% of revenue from DRAM/HBM test, with growing CoWoS test segment)
**Catalyst:** Q2 2024 earnings (July 2024) – expect commentary on HBM3e probe card orders from Samsung/SK Hynix and CoWoS test capacity expansion.
