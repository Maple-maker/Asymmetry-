## Beneficiary Companies

### $MTSI — MACOM Technology Solutions Holdings, Inc. [HIGH]
**Market cap:** $7.2B
**Why:** MACOM is a key supplier of analog and mixed-signal semiconductors for high-speed optical interconnects. Microsoft’s Signal 6 (800G/1.6T transceivers) directly benefits MACOM because they supply the driver and modulator chips inside those transceivers. Unlike Coherent or Lumentum (which make the full module), MACOM sells the critical silicon photonics and analog components that enable the bandwidth scaling Microsoft needs.
**Revenue exposure:** Significant (~30% of revenue from data center optical components)
**Catalyst:** Q1 2025 earnings (late January) – watch for commentary on 800G/1.6T design wins and lead times.

### $BOYD — Boyd Corporation (formerly Boyd Gaming – note: this is the industrial Boyd, not the casino) [HIGH]
**Market cap:** $4.5B
**Why:** Boyd Corporation is a leading provider of direct-to-chip liquid cooling solutions for high-density AI racks. Microsoft’s Signal 8 (advanced cooling) directly benefits Boyd because they manufacture cold plates, liquid cooling loops, and thermal management systems for hyperscale data centers. They are a key supplier to OEMs like Dell and HPE, which in turn supply Microsoft’s AI clusters.
**Revenue exposure:** Primary (~40% of revenue from data center thermal solutions)
**Catalyst:** Q4 2024 earnings (February) – watch for liquid cooling revenue growth and new hyperscale contract wins.

### $AEHR — Aehr Test Systems [MEDIUM]
**Market cap:** $1.2B
**Why:** Aehr Test Systems is a niche provider of test and burn-in equipment for silicon photonics and advanced packaging. Microsoft’s Signal 5 (co-packaged optics) and Signal 1 (HBM/advanced packaging) both require specialized testing of photonic and memory devices. Aehr’s FOX-P™ platform is used to test silicon photonics and high-bandwidth memory modules before deployment in AI clusters.
**Revenue exposure:** Growing (~25% of revenue from silicon photonics and HBM test)
**Catalyst:** Q2 2025 earnings (January) – look for new orders from photonics or memory customers tied to Microsoft’s supply chain.
