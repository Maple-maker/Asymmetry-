## Beneficiary Companies

### $SIMO — Silicon Motion Technology Corporation [HIGH]
**Market cap:** $2.8B
**Why:** Microsoft’s Signal 3 (SLM/Edge AI for Copilot+ PCs) requires high-performance, low-power NAND controllers for local AI model storage and retrieval. Silicon Motion is the dominant independent supplier of SSD controllers for client PCs (including Microsoft’s Surface line and OEM partners). As Copilot+ PCs require faster, higher-capacity SSDs to store and run on-device SLMs, SIMO’s controller revenue directly scales with this AI PC refresh cycle.
**Revenue exposure:** Significant (~40%+ of revenue tied to client PC SSD controllers, with AI PC uplift accelerating)
**Catalyst:** Q4 2024 earnings (late Jan 2025) – guidance for AI PC controller shipments and NAND flash pricing trends.

### $AOSL — Alpha and Omega Semiconductor Limited [MEDIUM]
**Market cap:** $1.1B
**Why:** Microsoft’s Signal 4 (liquid cooling for 100kW+ racks) and Signal 2 (high-speed interconnects) both require high-efficiency power management ICs and MOSFETs for server power stages and cooling pump drivers. AOSL is a niche supplier of GaN and silicon-based power semiconductors used in high-density data center power supplies and liquid cooling pump controllers. They have direct design wins with major ODM server makers supplying Microsoft’s AI clusters.
**Revenue exposure:** Growing (~20% of revenue from data center power, accelerating with liquid cooling adoption)
**Catalyst:** Fiscal Q2 2025 earnings (Feb 2025) – commentary on data center power revenue growth and GaN product ramp.

### $PLAB — Photronics, Inc. [HIGH]
**Market cap:** $1.5B
**Why:** Microsoft’s Signal 1 (HBM and advanced packaging capacity) directly drives demand for photomasks used in HBM memory and advanced interposer manufacturing. Photronics is the only pure-play public photomask company, supplying reticles for DRAM and logic packaging layers at Samsung and SK hynix fabs. As HBM3E and HBM4 require more mask layers per stack, PLAB’s revenue is a direct, overlooked proxy for HBM capacity expansion.
**Revenue exposure:** Significant (~30%+ of revenue from memory and advanced packaging photomasks)
**Catalyst:** Fiscal Q1 2025 earnings (mid-March 2025) – HBM-related mask orders and capacity utilization rates.

### $VIAV — Viavi Solutions Inc. [MEDIUM]
**Market cap:** $2.3B
**Why:** Microsoft’s Signal 2 (fiber optic transceiver tightness) requires advanced optical testing equipment for transceiver manufacturing and network validation. Viavi is the dominant supplier of optical test and measurement gear for 800G/1.6T transceiver production lines (used by Coherent, Lumentum, and Fabrinet). As Microsoft scales AI interconnects, Viavi’s test equipment orders are a leading indicator of transceiver volume.
**Revenue exposure:** Growing (~25% of revenue from optical test, directly tied to AI data center transceiver ramp)
**Catalyst:** Fiscal Q2 2025 earnings (late Jan 2025) – orders for 800G/1.6T test systems and data center customer commentary.

### $RPD — Rapid7, Inc. [MEDIUM]
**Market cap:** $2.5B
**Why:** Microsoft’s Signal 5 (EDR for Linux/macOS/IoT) highlights demand for cross-platform endpoint detection. Rapid7’s InsightIDR platform is a leading, independent EDR solution with strong Linux and IoT sensor coverage, directly competing with Microsoft Defender. Microsoft’s explicit statement that they are “buying best-in-class endpoint detection for Linux and IoT from specialized partners” positions Rapid7 as a potential acquisition target or preferred partner for Microsoft’s own security stack gaps.
**Revenue exposure:** Primary (~70%+ of revenue from cloud-delivered security, with growing Linux/IoT EDR)
**Catalyst:** Q4 2024 earnings (Feb 2025) – new large enterprise wins in Linux/IoT environments and any partnership announcement with Microsoft.
