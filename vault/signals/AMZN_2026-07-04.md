## Beneficiary Companies

### $FORM — FormFactor, Inc. [HIGH]
**Market cap:** $3.2B
**Why:** FormFactor is a leading supplier of advanced probe cards and test sockets used in the final test of HBM memory stacks and AI GPU packages. As Amazon buys more Nvidia H200/B200 GPUs and SK Hynix/Samsung HBM3E, the test interface market tightens. FormFactor’s proprietary MEMS-based probe cards are critical for yield management in high-bandwidth memory, and they have direct revenue from HBM test solutions.
**Revenue exposure:** Significant (~30% of revenue tied to memory and high-performance computing test)
**Catalyst:** Q4 2024 earnings (late January 2025) – guidance on HBM test card shipments and capacity utilization at memory fabs.
