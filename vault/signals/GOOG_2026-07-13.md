## Beneficiary Companies

### $SIMO — Silicon Motion Technology Corporation [HIGH]
**Market cap:** $2.8B
**Why:** Silicon Motion is the leading independent supplier of NAND flash controller ICs. As Google scales AI clusters with HBM and advanced DRAM, the demand for high-performance SSD controllers for data center caching and storage tiers (which buffer data to and from HBM) increases. Google’s TPU and GPU clusters require massive, low-latency storage, and Silicon Motion’s enterprise-grade controllers (e.g., for PCIe Gen5 SSDs) are a direct, non-obvious bottleneck component.
**Revenue exposure:** Significant (~30-40% of revenue from enterprise/cloud SSD controllers, heavily tied to hyperscaler procurement)
**Catalyst:** Q4 2024 earnings call (late January 2025) where management discusses hyperscaler design wins for next-gen data center SSDs.

### $LITE — Lumentum Holdings Inc. [HIGH]
**Market cap:** $4.5B
**Why:** Lumentum is a leading supplier of photonic components and optical transceivers. Google explicitly cited reliance on external suppliers for transceiver modules to meet AI cluster bandwidth demands. Lumentum’s high-speed coherent optical modules and silicon photonics-based transceivers are critical for the 800G/1.6T optical interconnects needed to link Google’s TPU pods across data centers.
**Revenue exposure:** Primary (~60%+ of revenue from cloud/data center optical networking)
**Catalyst:** Google’s next-generation TPU v6 deployment announcement (expected mid-2025) will require massive optical interconnect upgrades, directly benefiting Lumentum’s product cycle.

### $CLSK — CleanSpark Inc. [MEDIUM]
**Market cap:** $3.2B
**Why:** CleanSpark is a pure-play Bitcoin mining company that has pivoted into a high-density data center operator with expertise in power procurement and load balancing. Google’s need for 24/7 carbon-free energy (Signal 4) creates a direct opportunity for CleanSpark to sell its stranded or underutilized renewable energy capacity (solar + battery) to Google via PPAs or behind-the-meter arrangements, leveraging its existing interconnection queues.
**Revenue exposure:** Growing (~15-20% of future revenue could come from selling power capacity to hyperscalers, currently 100% mining)
**Catalyst:** Announcement of a PPA or colocation agreement with a hyperscaler (Google or AWS) for its Texas or Georgia renewable energy sites.

### $QBTS — D-Wave Quantum Inc. [MEDIUM]
**Market cap:** $1.1B
**Why:** D-Wave is the only publicly traded pure-play quantum computing company focused on annealing and gate-model systems. Google’s Signal 5 explicitly states it funds external research into error-corrected qubits and cryogenic control systems. D-Wave’s Advantage2 processor and its cryogenic control electronics (developed in-house) are directly relevant to Google’s need for external quantum hardware components and error-correction research partnerships.
**Revenue exposure:** Primary (~80%+ of revenue from quantum systems and related services)
**Catalyst:** Google’s Quantum AI team publishing a paper or announcing a partnership with D-Wave for error-correction or cryogenic control co-development.

### $TENB — Tenable Holdings, Inc. [HIGH]
**Market cap:** $5.2B
**Why:** Tenable is a leading provider of vulnerability management and exposure management solutions. Google’s Signal 6 highlights integration with third-party security vendors for endpoint detection and threat intelligence. Tenable’s “Tenable One” platform directly feeds vulnerability and exposure data into Google Cloud’s Security Operations (SecOps) suite, providing the unified view Google says it cannot build alone.
**Revenue exposure:** Significant (~40% of revenue from cloud-native vulnerability management, directly integrable with Google SecOps)
**Catalyst:** Google Cloud Next 2025 (April) where Tenable is announced as a premier integration partner for Chronicle SecOps.

### $OUST — Ouster, Inc. [MEDIUM]
**Market cap:** $1.0B
**Why:** Ouster is a leading manufacturer of solid-state lidar sensors for autonomous vehicles. Waymo (Google) explicitly states it works with multiple lidar suppliers to reduce cost and increase reliability. Ouster’s digital lidar (OS and DF series) offers the long-range, high-resolution, and low-cost per unit that Waymo needs for scaling its robotaxi fleet beyond current cities.
**Revenue exposure:** Primary (~70%+ of revenue from automotive and robotics lidar)
**Catalyst:** Waymo announcing a multi-year supply agreement with Ouster for its next-generation robotaxi platform (Gen 6).

### $ALAB — Astera Labs, Inc. [HIGH]
**Market cap:** $9.5B
**Why:** Astera Labs is a fabless semiconductor company specializing in connectivity solutions for AI infrastructure, including PCIe retimers and CXL memory controllers. Google’s Signal 7 mentions reliance on external partners for chiplet interconnect IP and custom ASIC design services. Astera’s Aries PCIe retimers and Leo CXL memory controllers are essential for connecting Google’s TPU chiplets and scaling memory pools in AI clusters.
**Revenue exposure:** Primary (~90%+ of revenue from AI data center connectivity silicon)
**Catalyst:** Google’s TPU v6 architecture reveal, which will likely require Astera’s PCIe Gen6 retimers for chiplet-to-chiplet communication.
