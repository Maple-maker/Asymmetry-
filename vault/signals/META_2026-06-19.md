## Beneficiary Companies

### $MTSI — MACOM Technology Solutions Holdings, Inc. [HIGH]
**Market cap:** $7.8B
**Why:** MACOM is a key supplier of analog and mixed-signal semiconductors for high-speed optical transceivers (400G/800G/1.6T) used in AI data center interconnects. Meta’s tight supply in high-speed optical transceivers (Signal 6) directly benefits MACOM’s laser drivers, modulators, and photodetectors, which are critical components in the optical modules that Coherent and Lumentum build. MACOM also supplies gallium nitride (GaN) power amplifiers for next-generation data center infrastructure, tying into Signal 2’s power infrastructure constraints.
**Revenue exposure:** Significant (~40% of revenue from data center optical and networking)
**Catalyst:** Q2 2025 earnings report (expected early May 2025) where management will likely guide on 1.6T transceiver ramp and AI-related orders.

### $VRT — Vertiv Holdings Co. [HIGH]
**Market cap:** $9.5B
**Why:** Vertiv is a pure-play provider of power and cooling infrastructure for data centers, directly addressing Meta’s capacity constraints in power infrastructure (Signal 2). Vertiv’s switchgear, busway, and precision cooling systems are essential for the electrical and mechanical buildout of Meta’s AI clusters. The company has a dedicated hyperscale customer segment and has explicitly cited Meta as a top customer in past filings.
**Revenue exposure:** Primary (~70% of revenue from data center power and thermal management)
**Catalyst:** Q1 2025 earnings (late April 2025) with hyperscale backlog updates; Meta’s 2025 infrastructure capex guidance of $60-65B directly supports Vertiv’s order pipeline.

### $LITE — Lumentum Holdings Inc. [HIGH]
**Market cap:** $5.2B

Lumentum also supplies silicon photonics engines for co-packaged optics (Signal 5), aligning with Meta’s evaluation of optical interconnect technologies to reduce power and latency.
**Revenue exposure:** Significant (~50% of revenue from telecom and datacom optical products)
**Catalyst:** Q3 2025 earnings (early May 2025) where AI transceiver revenue growth and 1.6T product sampling will be key metrics.

Aehr has disclosed contracts with multiple HBM memory manufacturers and silicon photonics foundries, making it a direct, overlooked beneficiary of Meta’s HBM and optical interconnect buildout.
**Revenue exposure:** Primary (~80% of revenue from semiconductor test, heavily tied to HBM and photonics)
