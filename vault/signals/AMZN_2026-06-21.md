## Beneficiary Companies

### $SIMO — Silicon Motion Technology Corporation [HIGH]
**Market cap:** $2.5B
**Why:** While not a direct HBM maker, Silicon Motion is the dominant supplier of SSD controllers for enterprise and data center storage. Amazon’s massive AI training clusters (H100/B200) require high-performance SSDs for checkpointing and data ingestion, and Silicon Motion’s PCIe Gen5 controllers are the bottleneck for these drives. Amazon is a top customer of SSD makers who use SIMO controllers.
**Revenue exposure:** Significant (estimated 30-40% of revenue tied to data center/enterprise SSD controllers)
**Catalyst:** Q1 2025 earnings call (late April) where data center controller revenue growth is reported.

### $AMKR — Amkor Technology, Inc. [HIGH]
**Market cap:** $6.8B
**Why:** Amkor is a leading OSAT (outsourced semiconductor assembly and test) provider specializing in 2.5D/3D advanced packaging. Amazon’s Trainium2/3 chips require exactly this type of packaging (silicon interposers, chiplets) to compete with Nvidia’s H100/B200. Amkor has a dedicated facility in Vietnam and Arizona for high-volume advanced packaging, and Amazon is a direct customer through its chip design partners.
**Revenue exposure:** Growing (advanced packaging revenue is ~20% of total, with Amazon as a key end customer)
**Catalyst:** Announcement of a multi-year packaging contract with a major hyperscaler (likely Amazon) at their Q1 2025 earnings.

### $RXRX — Recursion Pharmaceuticals, Inc. [MEDIUM]
**Market cap:** $1.2B
**Why:** Recursion is a clinical-stage biotech that uses AI for drug discovery. Amazon specifically called out healthcare AI agents for AWS Marketplace. Recursion has a deep partnership with AWS (using Trainium chips) and has already listed its proprietary AI models on AWS Marketplace for drug target identification. This is a direct, non-obvious vertical AI play.
**Revenue exposure:** Primary (Recursion’s entire business model is AI-driven drug discovery, with AWS as its core cloud partner)
**Catalyst:** Upcoming clinical trial readout (Phase 2 for REC-994) in Q2 2025, which will validate the AI model’s utility.

### $LITE — Lumentum Holdings Inc. [HIGH]
**Market cap:** $3.5B
**Why:** Lumentum is a leading manufacturer of high-speed optical transceivers (400G/800G) and silicon photonics components. Amazon’s data center networking is the largest consumer of these modules. Lumentum supplies the photonic chips and lasers used in the transceivers that Amazon buys from OEMs like Coherent and Cisco. Amazon’s quote about “heavily dependent on external optical module suppliers” directly benefits Lumentum’s component sales.
**Revenue exposure:** Significant (40%+ of revenue from cloud/data center optical components)
**Catalyst:** Q3 2025 earnings (early May) where 800G transceiver component shipments to Amazon are disclosed.

### $ASTS — AST SpaceMobile, Inc. [MEDIUM]
**Market cap:** $2.8B
**Why:** AST SpaceMobile is building a LEO satellite constellation for direct-to-cellphone connectivity. Amazon’s Project Kuiper needs optical inter-satellite links (OISL) and phased-array antennas. ASTS has developed its own OISL technology and has a supply chain for phased-array antennas that could be licensed or sold to Amazon. More directly, Amazon is a potential anchor tenant for ASTS’s ground station network.
**Revenue exposure:** Growing (ASTS’s entire business is LEO satellite communications; Amazon is a potential customer/partner)
**Catalyst:** Announcement of a commercial agreement with a major LEO operator (Amazon) for ground station or OISL components.

### $FLNC — Fluence Energy, Inc. [HIGH]
**Market cap:** $3.2B
**Why:** Fluence is a pure-play utility-scale battery storage integrator. Amazon explicitly stated it needs “utility-scale battery storage and long-duration storage solutions” for 24/7 data center load. Fluence’s Gridstack and Ultrastack products are directly deployable for AWS data centers. Amazon is already a major renewable energy buyer, and Fluence has won contracts with other hyperscalers (e.g., Google).
**Revenue exposure:** Significant (100% of revenue from energy storage; Amazon is a growing customer)
**Catalyst:** Q2 2025 earnings (May) where a multi-gigawatt-hour contract with a hyperscaler (Amazon) is announced.

### $QUBT — Quantum Computing Inc. [MEDIUM]
**Market cap:** $0.5B
**Why:** Amazon’s Braket service partners with quantum hardware startups. QUBT is a small-cap quantum computing company that provides quantum-ready software and hardware (entangled photon sources). It has a direct partnership with AWS for quantum optimization solutions. Unlike IonQ or Rigetti (which are more crowded), QUBT is overlooked and has a specific focus on quantum sensing and optimization for logistics—directly relevant to Amazon’s last-mile delivery and supply chain.
**Revenue exposure:** Primary (100% of revenue from quantum computing solutions; AWS is a key channel partner)
**Catalyst:** Launch of a new quantum optimization product on AWS Braket in Q2 2025.

### $WKHS — Workhorse Group Inc. [MEDIUM]
**Market cap:** $0.1B
**Why:** Amazon is expanding partnerships with smaller regional carriers and needs electric van conversion kits. Workhorse produces electric delivery vans and has a “C-Series” vehicle that is specifically designed for last-mile delivery. While Amazon has its own Rivian vans, Workhorse supplies conversion kits and chassis to smaller delivery service partners (DSPs) that Amazon contracts with. This is a direct, non-obvious play on Amazon’s “smaller independent delivery service partners” quote.
**Revenue exposure:** Primary (100% of revenue from electric delivery vehicles; Amazon DSPs are a target customer)
**Catalyst:** Q1 2025 earnings (May) where a new order from a major Amazon DSP is announced.
