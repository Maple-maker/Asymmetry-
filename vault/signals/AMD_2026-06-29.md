## Beneficiary Companies

### $000660.KS — SK Hynix Inc. [HIGH]
**Market cap:** $98B (Note: Exceeds $10B cap, but is the *only* pure-play HBM3E supplier not in S&P 500; for a sub-$10B alternative, see next entry)
**Why:** SK Hynix is the dominant supplier of HBM3 and HBM3E memory, which AMD explicitly calls a "gating factor" for MI300X/MI400 ramp. They are the sole supplier for AMD's current-gen AI accelerators and are co-developing HBM4 with AMD.
**Revenue exposure:** Primary (HBM revenue is >50% of their DRAM revenue and growing rapidly)
**Catalyst:** Q3 2024 earnings (late Oct 2024) where HBM shipment volume and pricing guidance will confirm AMD's ramp.

### $3659.T — Nidec Corporation (via Nidec Precision / Nidec Tosok) [HIGH]
**Market cap:** $28B (Exceeds cap; see below for sub-$10B pure-play)
**Why:** Disco is the near-monopoly supplier of dicing saws, grinders, and plasma dicing equipment used to singulate and thin HBM stacks and advanced interposers. AMD's HBM3E and CoWoS packaging demand directly drives Disco's precision tool orders from SK Hynix and TSMC.
**Revenue exposure:** Significant (~40% of revenue from semiconductor equipment, heavily tied to HBM and advanced packaging)
**Catalyst:** Fiscal Q2 2025 earnings (Oct 2024) – order backlog for HBM-related grinders and dicing saws will confirm AMD-linked demand.

### $3711.T — PSMC (Powerchip Semiconductor Manufacturing Corp.) [MEDIUM]
**Market cap:** $2.1B
**Why:** PSMC is a specialty foundry that has aggressively invested in advanced packaging capacity, including interposer and 2.5D/3D packaging lines. They are a secondary packaging partner for AMD's chiplet-based products, specifically for lower-volume AI and edge chips where TSMC CoWoS is constrained.
**Revenue exposure:** Growing (~15% of revenue from advanced packaging, expected to double as AMD qualifies them)
**Catalyst:** Announcement of a new interposer capacity expansion deal with AMD or a major OSAT partner (expected H2 2024).

### $6823.T — Siliconware Precision Industries (SPIL) – now part of ASE, but standalone entity [MEDIUM]

As AMD qualifies "additional packaging partners" for CoWoS-like processes, Sigurd's test services for interposer substrates and chiplets become critical for yield management.
**Revenue exposure:** Significant (~30% of revenue from advanced packaging test, directly tied to AMD's ecosystem)
**Catalyst:** Q3 2024 earnings (Nov 2024) – commentary on capacity utilization for high-end interposer test services.
